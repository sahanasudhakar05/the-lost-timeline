The Chronicle
of the Lost Architect

Long ago, an architect travelled across the world, studying the geometry hidden in art, monuments and decorative patterns. Before the Chronicle of Time could be completed, its archive shattered into four fragments.

Each fragment is concealed inside a motif. The equations have been scrambled. Identify the right mathematical tools, recover missing information, reject misleading segments and rebuild the designs.
